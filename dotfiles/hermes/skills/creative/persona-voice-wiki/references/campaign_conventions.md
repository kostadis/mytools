# Worked Example: Three-Campaign D&D Chronicle

User asked for an "LLM-style wiki in the voice of THE HISTORIAN OF CANDLEKEEP" from three campaign digests. Useful as a structural reference if the user requests more wikis of the same kind.

## Source digests (each with its own citation convention — PRESERVED verbatim)
- Stormgiants: `[ch: chapter_NN_descriptive_name.md]`
- Out of the Abyss: `(chapter_NN_descriptive_name.md)`
- Phandalin: `(chNN)` — note the *filename* is off-by-one from the in-file chapter header; citations use the filename.

## Deliverables (3 files)
1. `historian_chronology.md` — unified annotated chronology in the given campaign order, with a closing "Connecting Threads" section arguing the three campaigns are near-contemporaneous symptoms of one fraying planar age.
2. `historian_events.md` — 21 events (9 Stormgiants, 8 Abyss, 4 Phandalin). Each = cited factual summary + `**Historian's Assessment**` + signoff.
3. `historian_npcs.md` — 22 personages (11 Stormgiants, 9 Abyss, 2 Phandalin). Same structure.

## Persona mannerisms reused for voice consistency
- Opens with a precis epigraph: "*...*" — The Historian of Candlekeep.
- Refers to the Avowed (Candlekeep's order) with dry familiarity; admits institutional failings (the Candlekeep murder cover-up) without flinching.
- Cynical about patrons: the SEER, the Lords' Alliance, the Zhentarim are "claimants of the afterward."
- Recurring verdict phrase: "the age..." (e.g. "the age rewards the underground with clarity and the surface with chaos").
- Marks source unreliability explicitly (e.g. Orsik's unexplained "genius" from the headband).

## Counts that resulted (for spot-checking the technique)
- Chronology: 97 lines, 3,745 words, 2 signoffs (intro + closing).
- Events: 166 lines, 4,204 words, 21 Assessment blocks + 23 signoff lines (assessments + 2 closing).
- NPCs: 173 lines, 3,496 words, 22 Assessment blocks + 24 signoff lines.
