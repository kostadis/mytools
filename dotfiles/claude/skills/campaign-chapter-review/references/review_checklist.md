# Campaign Chapter Review Checklist

Use this checklist only when a review needs more structure than the main skill body.

## Canon

- Do route and location references match the chapter's place in the journey?
- Are unresolved mysteries kept unresolved?
- Are GM-only planning facts treated as pressure rather than party knowledge?
- Are item ownership and NPC statuses current?
- Are dates/seasons compatible with the canon docs?

## Voice

- Zalthir: terse, observational, decisive; warmth appears through noticing.
- Thorin: plain, physical, position/terrain oriented; feelings buried in concrete facts.
- Grygum: practical witness-priest; note-taking as faith; policy language applied deadpan.
- Daz: categorical, ledger-minded, journal-like; admits uncertainty only in small cracks.

## Artifacts

- Search for malformed transcript phrases.
- Check accidental player/table commentary that no longer belongs in prose.
- Check repeated motif overuse inside a single narrator scene.
- Check dialogue punctuation and quote attribution.

## Output Safety

- Write a sibling copy.
- Diff against original.
- Summarize only substantive edit categories.
